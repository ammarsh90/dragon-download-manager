# Branding assets

The Dragon Download Manager name and the custom artwork in `src/resources/logo/DragonDownloadManager.svg`, its generated app icons, and the matching Linux launcher icon are branding assets created for Brami Group in 2026.

The original application logo and legacy promotional screenshots have been removed. Original source copyright notices and the LGPL license remain intact.
